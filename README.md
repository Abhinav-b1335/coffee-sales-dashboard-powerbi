## Overview  
The Coffee Cafe Sales Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of sales performance, customer purchase behavior, and product category contributions. It helps stakeholders understand trends, identify top-performing products, and monitor store-level performance to improve decision-making and strategy.  

## Problem Statement  
Cafe owners and managers often struggle with fragmented data across locations and product types, making it difficult to analyze sales trends, optimize product offerings, and improve business performance. This dashboard consolidates sales data into a single interactive view, enabling quick insights.  

## Dataset  
- **Data Type**: Sales transactions data  
- **Time Period**: January 2023 – June 2023  
- **Fields Included**:  
  - Date & Time of Sale  
  - Store Location  
  - Product Category & Type  
  - Sales Amount  
  - Order Quantity  
  - Day/Week indicators  

## Tools and Technologies  
- Power BI – Dashboard creation & data visualization  
- Excel/CSV – Source data storage  
- DAX – Measures and calculations  

## Methods  
1. Data Cleaning & Transformation – Ensured consistency in sales records, standardized categories.  
2. DAX Measures – Created metrics like *Total Sales*, *Total Orders*, *Total Quantity Sold*.  
3. Visualizations – Used cards, line charts, bar charts, pie charts, and heatmaps to display KPIs.  
4. Filters & Slicers – Enabled monthly and weekday/weekend breakdowns.  

## Key Insights  
The dashboard provides insights across the period **Jan 2023 – Jun 2023** (with slicers to filter specific months):  

- **Sales Growth Trend**: Overall sales, orders, and quantity sold show consistent growth month-over-month.  
- **Product Category Insights**:  
  - Coffee and Tea dominate sales throughout the period.  
  - Bakery and Drinking Chocolate also show steady contributions.  
- **Product Type Insights**: Espresso-based drinks, Chai Tea, and Hot Chocolate remain customer favorites across months.  
- **Time-based Insights**:  
  - Peak hours are **8 AM – 12 PM** across all months.  
  - Weekdays contribute the majority of sales (~70–75%), showing reliance on daily commuters.  
- **Month-to-Month Variations**: Sales increase significantly in certain months (e.g., seasonal spikes), which can help identify demand patterns.  

## Dashboard / Output  
The dashboard includes:  
- KPI Cards for *Total Sales, Orders, Quantity Sold*.  
- Sales Trend Line Chart over May 2023.  
- Product Category & Product Type Breakdown.  
- Store Location Comparisons.  
- Day & Hour Heatmap for sales distribution.  
- Weekday vs Weekend Pie Chart.  

## Results & Conclusion  
The Coffee Cafe Sales Dashboard provides actionable insights into sales performance. The findings highlight:  
- Morning hours drive the highest sales.  
- Coffee and tea dominate revenue contributions.  
- Certain locations outperform others, guiding resource allocation.  
- Weekday sales significantly surpass weekend sales, suggesting weekday-driven business.
